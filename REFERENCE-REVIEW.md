# Reference website review

Reference: https://majd-portfolio.framer.website/#bio-section

## Design and components

- Off-white textured canvas; Archivo typography; black panels and oversized headings.
- Fixed rounded navigation with a logo and three-dot menu.
- Hero with a two-line software engineer heading, red portrait, metallic decorative shapes, copyright, and creating-since label.
- Biography, a large statement section, and four services: Website Migration, Framer Templates, Frontend Development, Product Consulting.
- Featured projects, testimonials, article cards, and a labeled contact form.
- Footer with the growth statement, quick links, and email contact.
- Work collection and six project detail pages with banners, descriptions, image galleries, and related projects.
- Blog collection and seven article pages with a banner, date, read time, body, newsletter signup, and related articles.

## Existing issues to edit

- The first article is titled “Building Trust Through Clear Design” but its body discusses vintage-car restoration. Several articles use sample material. Review all article bodies before presenting them as your own writing.
- Social links point to platform homepages rather than individual profiles.
- The “Get Started” link and project live links point to the reference creator's external destinations.
- The home article collection button says “View All Work” although it opens the blog.
- The contact textarea in the reference shares its `Email` field name with the email input. This recreation gives it a separate `Project` name to preserve both values.
- The reference includes template promotions and Framer branding; these were excluded from this personal portfolio version.

## What the recreation preserves

Rendered section markup, responsive page styling, reference imagery, fonts, content, and route slugs are retained. Images and fonts are cached locally. Editable data, named section components, a shared header, and custom interactions replace the site's hosted Framer runtime and analytics.

This is a close visual recreation, not a pixel-perfect guarantee across every browser and viewport. Framer-specific scroll choreography, per-letter animation, portrait flipping, and card animation timing are not reproduced exactly. Navigation uses an accessible dropdown. Project images have a light hover interaction; reduced-motion preferences are respected. Contact delivery and newsletter signup require your own recipient or endpoints.

No unrelated pages, admin panel, authentication, or additional portfolio features were added. The homepage and its existing linked pages make up the complete scope.
